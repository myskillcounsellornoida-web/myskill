Our Philosophy

Personalised, Student-First Support

Every student has a different story, pace, personality, and aspiration. At My Skill Counsellor, we reject one-size-fits-all programs. We focus on listening, understanding your goals, and crafting paths tailored specifically to your potential.

Whether navigating complex university applications, compiling statements of purpose, or preparing for language exams, we support both students and their families through every practical and emotional transition.

"As both a professional counsellor and the parent of an international student myself, I bring a unique balance of industry knowledge and real-world parent perspective to the table."

— Ria Jain, Founder
