My Journey & Philosophy

Hello, I am Ria Jain. I work closely with students and families through important academic, career, and study abroad decisions, helping them navigate higher education pathways with greater clarity, confidence, and direction.

My work involves supporting students through career exploration, profile building, university shortlisting, applications, and transition support. Over time, I have realised that guidance goes far beyond admissions alone. Every student has a unique story, pace, personality, and aspiration. My approach focuses on personalised, student-first support rather than one-size-fits-all counselling programs.

Having navigated the international education landscape both as a professional counsellor and as a parent of an international student myself, I bring a balance of professional experience and real-world understanding to the families I guide. I understand the excitement, the planning, and the emotional transitions that come with letting your child study abroad.
